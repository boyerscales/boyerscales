What we text back when someone asks "how much?"

These are the real replies Valley Details sends to price questions, pulled straight from our DMs, plus our price menu. Copy them and swap in your prices.

Elijah Boyer, co-owner of Valley Details in Elk Grove, CA

The one rule

Never send a price and stop. A price alone ends the conversation, and they go ask the next detailer. Every reply below ends with a question that moves them toward a time.

1. "How much for a detail?"

The most common message you get. Don't answer with a range.

STEP 1Ask for the car first

Hi how much would it be for my car?
Happy to help! What's the year, make and model? Then I can give you the exact price.

Why: the price depends on the size, so asking first gets you an exact number instead of a scary range. It also gets them typing back, and a person who's typing back is halfway booked.

STEP 2Exact price, then the next step

2019 Cruze
Got it! A Full Detail on a Cruze is $150, or $145 if we come to you. Want me to find you a time?

Why: one number for their exact car, plus the question that moves them to a time in the same message. No back and forth about "it depends".

IFThey only send a photo

[photo] how much?
Thanks for the photo! I can't tell the exact model from a picture, so what's the year, make and model? Then I'll give you the price right away.

2. "What's your cheapest option?"

What's you guys's cheapest option
The cheapest is the Express at $50 for a sedan, about an hour. Good if it just needs a wipe down and a quick wash, not for pet hair, spills or ground-in dirt. The Full Detail is $150. Which one would you like?

Why: give them the cheap one honestly and say what it's not for. The right customers book it, and the people with a trashed car pick the bigger job on their own.

3. "How much for just the interior?"

how much for just the interior?
Interior Only is $85 for a sedan, $95 for an SUV and $105 for a truck or 3-row, and $5 less each if we come to you. What's the year, make and model so I can give you the exact one?

Why: they asked a direct question, so give all three sizes at once, then ask for the car. They see you're straight with prices and keep going.

4. "It's not that big though"

When they push back on the size tier.

Well it's a mid size suv tho not as big as an Escalade
Totally fair, it's not Escalade size! Our sizing goes by seating though, and with a third row it lands in our largest tier. We confirm the size when we see it. Want me to find you a time?

Why: agree with them first, then give a simple rule they can check themselves (seats, not looks). "We confirm when we see it" takes the pressure off, and the question brings it back to booking.

5. Stains, pet mess, "can you get this out?"

what about dog pee, its just on the driver seat
For that, Interior Only is the right one, the Express won't cut it. If it soaked into the seat it usually needs a shampoo, which is extra, so we'll look at it and tell you what it needs before we start. Want me to find you a time?

Why: never quote a stain job over text. Point them to the right service, be upfront that it might cost more, and promise no surprises. "Before we start" is the line that makes them trust you.

6. "That's out of my budget"

I will have to pass as it's out my budget. Weekly is a little much
Totally understand, thanks for letting us know! Whenever you want one for the season, just message us here.

Why: don't discount and don't beg. Leave the door open and keep the thread. They come back when the car gets bad enough, and you're the one they already talked to.

7. They say yes. Lock the time.

STEP 1Mobile or drop-off, plus real times

Yes please what times work
Tomorrow has every start from 8am to 6pm open. Would you like us to come to you, or would you rather drop it off?

STEP 2Get the number

Drop it off. 8 am works for me
Sounds good, drop-off tomorrow at 8am. Can I get your first name and mobile number for your confirmation text?

Why: repeat the time back so there's no confusion, then ask for the number "for your confirmation text". Nobody says no to that, and now you can text a reminder the day before so they actually show up.

Bonus: answer in whatever language they write in. Our assistant has answered price questions in Spanish, Punjabi and Russian.
