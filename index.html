01Get found

Look like the obvious choice before anyone calls.

Most people pick a detailer off a map result or a quick search. What they see there, and on your site right after, decides whether they reach out or keep scrolling.

A Google profile set up by the rules

For Valley Details I wrote the profile to spec: the right primary category, service areas instead of a home address, real prices on every service, and a plain business name. Stuffing keywords into the name is a common way new profiles get suspended.

A website search engines can actually read

Services, prices and cities in plain text. Structured markup that tells search engines this is a detailing business, where it works, and when it's open. Fast on a phone, because that's where the search happens.

A steady flow of new reviews

Every finished job gets asked, by text, while the car still looks new. More recent, real reviews means more trust with the people comparing you side by side.

The honest version

Nobody can promise you the top spot on Google, and I won't. Google says local results weigh relevance, distance and prominence, and reviews are part of prominence. What I can do is make sure your profile and site are complete, accurate and consistent, and that good customers get asked for reviews every time. That builds trust with people either way, and it can help you show up.

02Get booked

Close the gap between “looks good” and “I'm booked.”

When someone lands on your site, the interest is already there. The job is not to lose them. Every screen on Valley Details points at one action.

  1. 1

    The price is on the first screen

    $100, inside and out, no deposit. The question most people would call to ask is answered before they scroll.

  2. 2

    Call and Book never leave the screen

    On phones, both buttons stay pinned to the bottom. Whenever they're ready, they don't have to hunt.

  3. 3

    Pick a time first. Details second.

    The service, the day and the time come first, so it reads like a calendar, not a form. Name, phone and car only show up after a time is tapped.

  4. 4

    Real availability

    Times come from the live schedule. Taken slots show crossed out instead of disappearing, so the calendar never offers a time that's gone.

  5. 5

    Permission to text, asked at the right moment

    A checkbox at booking lets customers opt in to open-slot deals. That list is what the rest of the system markets to.

Right after they book

It becomes a customer, not a notification.

The booking lands in the dashboard as a person with the service, time and phone number. The confirmation text goes out from the business's own number, and the owner can get an alert.

Plans change

Customers move their own appointment.

Every confirmation carries a private link to that one appointment. No phone tag to reschedule, and the calendar stays right.

When they call instead

A missed call doesn't have to be a missed job.

For shops that can't answer mid-detail, an AI receptionist can pick up, take the details and book. If a call ends without a booking, a text follows up on its own.

03Follow up · Blue SMS

Texts from your number. Not a no-reply robot.

Blue SMS sends through the business's own iPhone and number. Customers get a normal text thread with the business, blue bubbles on an iPhone, and their replies come back to that phone and into the dashboard. The follow-up that happens is the one nobody has to remember.

    Built with the brakes on

    Automated texting done carelessly burns a customer list. These rules are in the code.

    • opt-inPromos only go to people who said yes.Every promo is checked against a recorded YES and no STOP before it sends.
    • 7 daysOne promo a week, at most.Per customer, no matter how many campaigns are running.
    • bookedNothing while they're already booked.No “come back” text to someone who's on the calendar.
    • 10a–6pPromos go out during the day.Between 10 AM and 6 PM, never at night.
    • repliedA reply pauses the sequence.Once they answer a promo, rebook and win-back texts wait until their next detail.
    • onceEach reminder sends exactly once.A restart or a double tap can't text a customer twice.

    04Bring them back

    A finished detail is the best marketing you'll get. Use it.

    A happy customer standing next to a clean car is proof, a referral source, and your next booking. It's easy to let all three drive off.

    Reviews

    Turn completed details into more proof.

    When a job is marked complete, the customer gets a thank-you text with the review link while the car still looks new. It's guarded so it can't send twice.

    So far: Valley Details has a 5.0 rating from 8 Google reviews.

    What we never do: trade discounts for reviews. Google prohibits it and can filter those reviews. Ask every customer, never pay for it.

    Hi Marcus, glad the car came out right. Your link gives friends $20 off their first detail with Valley Details, and you get $20 off your next one when they book: valleydetails.site/?ref=…
    Later, a friend books with the link
    Hi Marcus, Dana just got detailed with Valley Details using your link. Your $20 off is waiting and comes off automatically next time you book: valleydetails.site

    Referrals

    Give happy customers something to share.

    Every customer gets their own link. Their friend gets $20 off the first detail. When that friend's job is done, the customer's $20 comes off their next booking automatically. The owner sees each customer's link, who they sent, and the credit left.

    Then the calendar does the remembering.

    After a detail, the system knows when each customer is due and texts them at the right time. These are the default timings. Each business sets its own, and every promo follows the rules above.

      New offers

      Launch a membership without a separate system.

      Valley Details launched the Valley Club: two full details a month for $149, with ten founding spots. The site takes signups, the spots-left counter is real, and the system can pitch the Club by text a few days after a customer's detail.

      Open slots

      Fill tomorrow's gaps with people who already like you.

      From noon, if tomorrow still has openings, opted-in past customers can get an open-slot deal. It stops the moment tomorrow fills up.

      Honest offers

      A promo that says $20 off is honored automatically.

      When that customer books, the job saves at the lower price with a note explaining why. The crew knows, and the numbers in the dashboard stay true.

      The owner's side

      Never lose track of someone who wants a detail.

      None of this helps if the owner can't see it. Every booking, call and text lands in one place, so the team knows who the customer is, what they want, and what happens next.

      • Who's booked and who isn't. Every person is Booked, Not booked or Completed, so nobody who asked about a detail disappears.
      • Text them from their record. The whole Blue SMS thread lives on the customer, sent from the business's number.
      • Mark it complete, and the review ask goes out. One tap closes the job and starts the after-detail texts.
      • Alerts when something happens. New lead or new booking, sent where the owner will see it.
      • Built for a phone. It installs to the home screen and works one-handed between jobs.
